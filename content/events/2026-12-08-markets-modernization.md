This time, we're bringing together two very different stories that share something in common: challenging assumptions and finding better ways to build software.

Can AI-powered trading bots really beat Wall Street? And what does it take to modernize a long-lived, complex cloud platform without getting lost in years of accumulated complexity?

Join us for two talks full of experimentation, engineering challenges, lessons learned, and real-world experiences.
